Rocky's Personal Lab

Since my childhood I've dreamed of countless different simulations in my brain, but now I have the power to make them happen, and share it to anyone. Now that I have child of my own, I can help him realize all of his thoughts - and it won't even take as much time as I took to write these, as most of them were pre-LLM passion projects.

Conway's Game of Life
Simulation

A browser implementation of Conway's cellular automaton with seeding, controls, and run statistics.
Cellular Automata
Simulation tool

Load and run cellular automata from short JSON descriptions, with included examples for forests, mazes, waves, and more.
Hydrology Simulator
Simulation game

A desktop-oriented hydrology sandbox for inspecting terrain, precipitation, basins, aquifers, flora, and climate changes.
Markov Generator
Interactive text tool

Generate text from seed passages using a weighted Markov chain, with sample corpora included.
Prehistoric Simulation
Simulation game

A local browser simulation game about map generation, people, resources, and settlements.
